# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

MarketMind is a technical research project. Interactions should be:

- **Intellectually honest** — challenge ideas, not people.
- **Evidence-based** — back claims with data, citations, or reasoning.
- **Constructive** — criticism should improve the work.
- **Respectful** — disagree without being dismissive.

## Expected Behaviour

- Critique technical decisions, not the people who made them.
- Acknowledge when you are wrong — it makes the research better.
- Ask questions before assuming bad intent.
- Document disagreements in ADRs or issues, not in passive-aggressive code comments.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Personal attacks, insults, or harassment.
- Dismissing valid technical concerns without engaging with them.
- Deliberately introducing errors or bias into the research pipeline.
- Misrepresenting research findings.

## Enforcement

Violations may result in removal from the project.
